# Current limitations — v0.2

- Both engines are research prototypes. No species-specific calibration, independent simulator validation, held-out empirical test, or powered confirmatory comparison has been completed. Reproducibility and invariant tests do not prove biological realism or a universal reproductive advantage.
- The fixed-census reference remains an E1 analytical control and cannot estimate ecological extinction/invasion. The demographic engine can under its stated synthetic life cycle. Steps in the latter are overlapping census intervals, not independent genetic generations.
- There is one shared patch per run and no spatial migration, sex allocation or male/female class. Resource/age/selection parameters have arbitrary units. Survival and resource/census lotteries are common to programs as declared.
- Costs match expected focal effort, not a hard per-donor energy budget. Contributors can be reused without individual debits. Hypothetical chromosome partition discards gamete material without a calibrated nutrient cost. Zero-cost and contributor-surcharge cases are explicit model sensitivities.
- Both four/eight-parent mechanisms are inventions. The locus mosaic reserves allele slots to guarantee contribution, changing the unconstrained distribution. Chromosome partition enforces whole-chromosome contribution and diploid dosage without demonstrating feasible fusion/reduction or historical occurrence.
- Facultative mixing-bout probability is a quantitative modifier inherited from the focal breeder outside selected nuclear loci. Program labels do not mutate between complete programs. Designed introductions condition on a compatible cohort; origins of reproductive machinery and gradual two→four→eight pathways are not modeled.
- Founder ages are not a fitted stable-age distribution. Binary loci, independent founder alleles, symmetric mutation, target-dependent dominance, uniform recombination without interference/conversion and simple multiplicative selection limit genetic realism.
- Nonstatic environments also vary resource renewal, combining two ecological changes. Parasites are synthetic bit-vector selection pressure, not independently reproducing pathogens. Further factorial controls are needed to separate these effects.
- Mean performance is not reproductive-value-weighted lifetime fitness. Adaptive lag and D² have narrow definitions in `ECO_MODEL.md`. Shares and log growth have explicit survival conditioning. Population-wide extinction leaves shares undefined.
- Eight replicates yield imprecise exploratory uncertainty. Bootstrap intervals are descriptive/unadjusted; exact paired sign tests use Holm within a scenario only. The full grid is not a confirmatory family or an equivalence test. Crossing an introduction threshold is not fixation or permanent establishment.
- Full candidate parentage records include contribution counts, funding and recruitment, but not exact crossover paths or mutation events. Extinction times are integer census times, not within-step times. Initially absent programs are not in the risk set; zero is absorbing without reintroduction.
- Same-environment checkpoints reject changed source/protocol/runtime/config. Cross-platform bitstream identity and v0.1 checkpoint migration are not promised. Interrupted attempts are preserved and successful runs verified before batch resume.
- Full lineage is retained in RAM and checkpoint compression grows with history. Generic compatibility uses a greedy sequential search that can fail despite another feasible group. Reference weighted sampling is O(N²); the common ecological anchored sampler is O(births × k²). Safety limits raise errors instead of silently clipping offspring or dropping logs. Extreme reference weights may underflow and trigger a support error.
- CSV/JSON, deterministic gzip lineage, NPZ checkpoints, PNG/SVG figures and local HTML/Markdown reports are implemented. YAML/Parquet, compressed genealogical trees, continuous space and calibrated population/pathogen models remain future work. No million-individual experiment has run.
