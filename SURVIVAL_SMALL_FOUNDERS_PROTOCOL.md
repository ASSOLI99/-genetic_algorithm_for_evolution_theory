No initial audit run or analysis is replaced.

Seeds are the first six prespecified audit seeds, 41001..41006, chosen to fit the remaining workload budget, without selecting based on their results. The other six programs are initially absent and are not in extinction risk sets. Checkpoints at 400 and 800; full ledgers, same independent accounting/digest checks.

Founder genomes/ages are sampled from the same distributions, not asserted to be the same individual IDs/genotypes as in a larger founder pool. Per-environment results have six independent seeds, Wilson intervals and right censoring at 800; this is a small exploratory control. Cases/environments share seeds and are not pooled as independent replicates. There is no species/time calibration or universal reproductive claim. Engine code is unchanged.
